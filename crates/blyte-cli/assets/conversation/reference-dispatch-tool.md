These are facts about how you delegate in this run. They say what the tool does, not how to run your work.

You delegate through the `dispatch` tool, not with `blyte dispatch` in the shell. Each call starts one
implementer on one task: `task` is the whole of what the implementer is told, and `gate` is the
command that decides whether the task is done. The call returns when the implementer's run has ended,
with its report.
